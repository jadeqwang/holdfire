# HOLD FIRE — music video

*Song: "Hold Fire" by Jade Wang with Claude, from the album* Rare Earth. *The words were found in Jade's poetry (the*
Spaces Left Blank *trilogy and the 2009 chapbook* Variations and Metamorphosis*) and then rhymed.*

## Take two

> A history of restraint: the Daoist alchemists who wrote gunpowder down as a method to avoid, Petrov in 1983 and Arkhipov
> in 1962, with anime Jade walking through every era while each era is drawn in its own art style. The second voice is
> teal-tips Jade in a rival control room, her mirror image, and the reason to hold fire is the Rare Earth contact graph:
> six hundred worlds that we only reach if we are still here.

Unlike take one, the generated pictures are shown: stills and video (Nano Banana Pro, Seedance, Veo) are graded and
composited, and JavaScript paints over them (blueprint linework, riso and halftone screens, brushed type, VHS rewind, impact
frames, data displays). Everything that is data rather than a person stays procedural (three.js WebGL2 and Canvas 2D,
rendered frame by frame in headless Chromium).

### How it was made

1. **Timing.** Take one's timing map (`analysis/timing.json` = `render/data/timing.json`: beats, word onsets, hits) is the master
   clock, unchanged. Every cut is placed on a beat, a word or a hit; `render/tools/check_cuts.mjs` fails any cut more than one
   frame off its onset.
2. **Sheets.** A teal-tips Jade turnaround matched to the DOT sheet (`design/take2/teal_tips_sheet.jpg`), both control rooms (B is
   A mirrored, cooler light), the dish field and the mirror void, and one style frame per era (blueprint, Okavango gouache,
   signal print, ink wash, Petrov oil, chess, poster). Every keyframe is made from a character sheet plus a set or style frame.
3. **Plates.** Keyframes in Nano Banana Pro, then Seedance 2.5 (sung shots, 720p, the vocal-stem slice as the lip-sync
   reference), Veo 3.1 or Seedance 2.0 (unsung motion), or the still itself with a JS camera. `tools/t2/plates.py` conforms each
   pick to 24 fps frames; the renderer's `t2_plate` / `t2_still` / `t2_split` show them on twos, graded, under a style pass
   (riso, halftone, newsprint, ink, oil, gouache, blueprint, VHS) and JS overlays (blueprint schematics drawn on the snares,
   Petrov's screen mapped onto the painted wall display behind his head, impact frames, speed lines, smash zooms). No
   model-made lettering: every word and number on screen is drawn by the renderer.
4. **Procedural scenes.** Take one's pulsar plot, Golden Record cover, coins and phyllotaxis disk; Rare Earth's galaxy and contact
   graph, war and night-side Earth (with a VHS rewind); new: the chess game (board built from the PGN, every frame a legal
   position, `chess_check.mjs`), the near-miss teletype, the coin tree, the reset counter (a roller counter: low wheels roll, high
   ones stay at 0), the CRT wall, brushed characters and the warning scroll, ink weapons, the Suprematist morph and the
   Arkhipov poster.
5. **Lyrics.** Every sung line, shout and whisper is on screen while it is sung, set into the world of its era
   (`render/src/lyrics2.js`): blueprint caps and words pressed into clay in verse 1, riso over space, gold light in the sky,
   CRT phosphor in the control rooms, brush Latin in the ink wash, an amber terminal for Petrov, ticker tape for the list,
   engraved gold on the coins, chalk at the board, impact slams in the battle, poster banners for Arkhipov, VCR lettering on the
   rewind, marker on the mirror glass. Jade's rules: no punctuation, no exclamation marks, ≥ 0.6 s a word, never over a face.
6. **The mirror rule.** Teal-tips is Jade's true mirror image: her shots are generated in the canon jacket and flipped
   left-right in the renderer, so her dot and 1420 MHz patch sit on her right, her room is Jade's mirrored and her lettering
   reads reversed. Jade's world is never flipped. Two-shots (DR03, DR04, DR11, DR16) are not flipped; teal-tips wears the
   mirrored jacket in their keyframes.
7. **Lip sync.** For every sung take the lag was measured on an ECC-tracked mouth box, scored as played on twos
   (`tools/sync/lipmeasure.py`, `mouthline.py`), and `render/tools/lipcheck.mjs` confirmed the renderer draws those frames.
   Then Jade picked by eye and ear on a lip-sync page: 13 offsets per sung shot (−24 to +24 frames, even, so the twos phase
   holds), and her pick k became `lag − k/24` in the picks (14 shots, plus retakes of V101 and DR16). Source:
   [`tools/t2/lipsync/`](tools/t2/lipsync/).
8. **Review.** Each preview went onto a review page (an HLS player with time-stamped notes stored in the page, v1 to v6;
   [`tools/t2/review/`](tools/t2/review/)); Jade's notes and the fixes are in `REVIEW_NOTES.md`. An outside model (Gemini)
   watched preview v3 in five chunks; of its 58 claims, 18 checked out against the frames, and polish pass 4 fixed those (lip
   sync aside, which went to the picker). Contact sheets every
   0.25–0.5 s after each polish pass, and a 1080p QA of each release candidate (`QA_RC1.md`, `QA_RC2.md`).

### Reproduce

```sh
python3 tools/t2/merge_picks.py                          # docs/take2/picks_*.json -> render/data/picks2.json
python3 tools/t2/plates.py media/gen/<take>.mp4 <PICK>   # once per pick (its src in picks2.json; stills: media/kf2/*.jpg --fit keep)
python3 tools/t2/credits.py                              # end-card credits from the picks the edit uses
cd render
node tools/check_cuts.mjs && node tools/chess_check.mjs && node tools/chess_lens.mjs && node tools/lipcheck.mjs
node tools/render.mjs --stills 13.5,95.5,246.5 --w 1920 --h 1080 --outdir ../work/stills/t2
cd .. && python3 tools/audio/sfx_t2.py                  # the mix (re-run after any timing change)
tools/t2/render_segments.sh hold_fire_t2_master_rc4 1920 1080 14 media/audio/hold_fire_t2_mix.wav   # restart-safe, 12 s segments
cd work/out                                           # X file (pipeline_lessons §3.7)
X="-c:v libx264 -b:v 12M -maxrate 16M -bufsize 24M -profile:v high -level:v 4.1 -refs 4 -preset slow -tune grain -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 24"
nice -n 10 ffmpeg -i hold_fire_t2_master_rc4.mp4 $X -pass 1 -an -f mp4 /dev/null
nice -n 10 ffmpeg -i hold_fire_t2_master_rc4.mp4 $X -pass 2 -c:a aac -b:a 256k -movflags +faststart hold_fire_t2_x.mp4
# hook: the same two passes with -t 31 -vf fade=t=out:st=30.5:d=0.5 -af afade=t=out:st=30.5:d=0.5 (audio on pass 2)
```

The plates in `work/plates/` (2.5 GB) and the generated takes in `media/gen/` (1.3 GB) are not committed. The 1080p render
takes about 55 minutes with 3 jobs on 4 CPUs (0.55 s a frame). With no `media/audio/hold_fire_mix.wav` present,
`render.mjs` muxes `inputs/Hold Fire.mp3`, so the take-two master carries the song itself (no sound-design layer).

## Take one

> A hunter's crosshair settles on a small world that is counting in light, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and the hunter does not
> fire. Three minutes later we learn who the hunter was, and that the count it heard was answered by six hundred and
> ten worlds that also held their fire.

Every frame is drawn by JavaScript (three.js WebGL2 and Canvas 2D, rendered frame by frame in headless Chromium). The
style is **Golden Record**: the film is drawn the way humanity wrote to the stars (the Pioneer plaque, the Voyager
Golden Record, the Arecibo message), in lines of gold light engraved on black. Generated video (Seedance 2.5) and stills
(Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image) are used only as rotoscope reference. The renderer reads them as guide maps (tone, flow,
mattes, face landmarks) and redraws everything; the footage itself never appears.

```
        └──► guide maps: engraving streamlines, constellations, stipple, mattes, faces  │
                  └──► renderer: scenes + kinetic type + film compositor, renderAt(t) ◄─┘
                            └──► headless Chromium ──► ffmpeg ──► + sound-design mix
```

1. **Timing** (`tools/audio/`, `analysis/timing.md`). Stems by BS-RoFormer; four forced alignments plus two
   speech-to-text passes, every word snapped to a vocal onset and checked by hand; a beat grid that follows the
   tempo drift (93 to 95.5 BPM); analysed events (drums out, impacts, rolls). Every cut in
   [`render/src/timeline.js`](render/src/timeline.js) is placed on a word, a beat or an event, never on a typed number.
2. **Plates** (`tools/design/`, `tools/sd/`). Character and set sheets, then a first-frame keyframe per shot composed
   with room for the type, then Seedance 2.5 in first-frame mode with the vocal-stem slice as the audio reference.
   49 shots, 1–6 takes each; lip-sync lags picked by eye from mouth-shape strips (`tools/sync/lipsync.py`), because
   correlation metrics lie on sustained notes.
3. **Guides** (`tools/roto/`). Per picked take: structure-tensor flow and Jobard–Lefer streamlines for the engraving,
   CLAHE tone, a person matte, MediaPipe face landmarks (the mouth is carved from them so the sync reads), constellation
   stars with stable ids, stipple colour.
4. **Renderer** (`render/`). A deterministic `renderAt(t)`: scenes draw into a float scene buffer, the kinetic type
   layer is composited over a film compositor (bloom, halation, streaks, grain, grade). Procedural scenes for
   everything that is data rather than a person: the snowflake (a Reiter cellular model), the pulsar plot, the Golden
   Record cover, Oko's polar map (Natural Earth), the near-miss list, 100 coins, a 58k-star Milky Way from the Reid
   arms, the 610-world phyllotaxis graph.
5. **Sound** (`tools/audio/sfx.py`). A synthesized layer (no samples) of glass pings in D minor on the counts, the
   scope, a match strike, wind, sonar. It sits at least 6 dB under the vocal wherever a word sounds; the mix is
   bit-identical to the song outside the cue windows and within 0.01 LU of its loudness.
6. **Review**. Storyboards, 1 fps contact sheets, and an outside model (Gemini) watching each 35–50 s window of the
   cut for dead shots, illegible type and missed beats; its notes and the fixes are in [`docs/reviews/`](docs/reviews/).

### Reproduce

```sh
cd render
node tools/render.mjs --stills 7.5,60,200 --w 1920 --h 1080 --outdir ../work/stills/x      # stills
node tools/storyboard.mjs ../work/board.jpg                                              # one frame per shot
node tools/render.mjs --from 0 --to 249.16 --w 1920 --h 1080 --jobs 3 --out ../work/out/hold_fire.mp4
```

The renderer needs the guide maps in `work/guides/` (about 3 GB, not committed; regenerate with
`tools/roto/prep.py` from the takes listed in `render/data/picks.json`).
