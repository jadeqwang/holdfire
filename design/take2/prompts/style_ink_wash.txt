A Chinese ink-wash painting on rice paper: a misty mountain grotto with gnarled pines on the cliff and a waterfall, four adult Daoist alchemists in fine-line robes, as tall as she is and standing at the same distance from the viewer, gathered around a bronze tripod cauldron, smoke curling up from it, mortar and pestle, sparse expressive brushwork, wide areas of empty paper, a few touches of cinnabar red. Teal-tips Jade stands among them at the centre of the frame, just behind the cauldron, large in the frame (her figure from head to knees fills about two thirds of the frame height), three-quarter front view, singing, mouth open on a long note, eyes on the smoke, her hands empty and relaxed (no microphone, nothing in her hands), face clearly visible and near the middle of the picture: she is the only cel-shaded figure in the painting, in clean flat colour. The painting fills the whole frame edge to edge as one continuous scroll painting, with mist and empty paper in the upper right; no panels, no borders, no seams.
Teal-tips Jade, the girl in the TEAL reference sheet, stays flat cel-shaded anime with crisp black lineart, unlike the world around her, exactly on model: Jade's face, shoulder-length straight black hair with no bangs whose lower third fades to bright teal, brown eyes, ONE pair of orange-foam headphones worn over her ears (nothing around her neck), the same cropped white jacket with orange bands and a pale-blue left-chest dot, black high-neck crop top, navy cargo trousers.
No calligraphy, no characters, no seals; the alchemists are anonymous, faces small and stylised.
Palette: ink black, grey wash, rice paper, cinnabar.
