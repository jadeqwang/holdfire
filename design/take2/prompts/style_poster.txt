A Soviet constructivist poster in the Suprematist manner, printed in coarse newsprint halftone on cream paper, red, black and cream only: a Foxtrot-class diesel submarine (B-59) in flat black silhouette surfacing at night through a heaving sea drawn as bold diagonal red and black bands, a huge black square behind it and a sharp red wedge slicing diagonally across the composition toward it; small black circles (depth charges) falling from the top of the frame; tiny flat silhouettes of three sailors on the conning tower, one with an open raised hand. Bold geometry, strong diagonals, flat colour, visible halftone dots and slight misregistration.
