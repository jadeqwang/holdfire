## For the site's fact-check

**In the film, not on the site (16):** 1950 BC (B-36 jettison), 1957 Kyshtym, 1958 Tybee Island, 1958 Mars Bluff, 1962 Vandenberg (Atlas test at DEFCON 2), 1965 Ticonderoga, 1968 K-129, 1968 USS Scorpion, 1973 DEFCON 3, 1979 Sverdlovsk anthrax, 1980 Grand Forks B-52 fire, 2022 BrahMos misfire, May 2025 India–Pakistan strikes, Sep 2025 drones over Poland, the 2026 AI false-intelligence report (reported, approximate date) and the Sep 2026 Indian–Pakistani warship collision. Each has its own source and date note in the JSON.

**Every site entry is in the film** (all 18).

**Two dates differ.** Jade's decision (30 Oct): the film keeps the explosion date for Damascus and the flight date for Minot, so the site should change to match:
- 1980-09-19: site says 18 Sep (the socket was dropped that evening; the explosion came about 03:00 on 19 Sep). The film uses the explosion date.
- 2007-08-30: site says 29 Aug (the missiles were loaded that day; the flight was 30 Aug). The film uses the flight date.

**The corrections you listed are consistent with the film:** the 3 Jun 1980 false warning (the film's line doesn't name Brzezinski); the 1967 solar storm (the film's line says only that it jammed the radars); Able Archer from 7 Nov; Warren 2010 at 45 minutes; Okinawa (28 Oct 1962) is not on the list. The coin grid does stamp twelve more take-one dates as unverified, with a small "?" under the year, and Okinawa and the 1956 Suez alarm are among them (years only, no text).
