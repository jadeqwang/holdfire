Plain mid-grey seamless studio backdrop, soft even studio light, sharp focus. Layout: top row, four full-body views of the same being at identical scale (front, three-quarter, side profile, back), with a plain grey 168 cm human silhouette beside the front view for scale. Bottom row: three close-ups of its face (calm; curious, antennae raised; afraid, antennae flattened back) and one close-up of its long three-fingered hand.
Keep the design of the reference image (the deep pale cream and sage-green fur ruff, the feathered gold antennae, the large dark eyes ringed in gold, the two pairs of folded translucent wings with circular eye-spots and long trailing hindwing tails), but make it more dignified, ancient and graceful, and less cartoonish:
- It is tall (about 220 cm) and slender like a heron, with a long elegant neck; its whole body, arms and legs are covered in soft, short, pale velvet fur (no bare grey skin), with fine darker markings along the limbs.
- Its face is a soft, short-furred moth face, wider at the eyes and tapering gently; the two large dark glossy eyes are set wide apart, each with a thin luminous gold ring around the pupil; there is no cartoon mouth, only a small soft fold of fur below the eyes; its expressions come from the eyes, the brow fur and the antennae.
- Long slender arms ending in long, delicate three-fingered hands with soft pads; it holds them close to its chest.
- The folded wings hang down its back like a long cloak to the ground; the eye-spots on the forewings are made of concentric rings of cream, black and luminous gold.
It is gentle, careful, intelligent and a little shy: a person, not a pet and not a monster.
