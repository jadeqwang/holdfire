Environment design sheet, photoreal concept art for an original live-action science-fiction film (in the visual language of Interstellar, Arrival and Annihilation: vast, quiet, low-key, believable). An inhabited ocean world 89 light-years from Earth. Four panels in a 2x2 grid, separated by thin dark gutters:
1) THE SEA AT TWILIGHT: a calm black ocean with faint blue-green phosphorescence in the waves, tall dark sea stacks, and a colossal pale ringed gas giant filling a third of the sky, its rings a thin bright arc.
2) THE LISTENING POST: on top of a lone sea stack, a long, slender, crystalline instrument about 30 metres long, half telescope and half weapon, mounted on a cradle and aimed at one point among the stars; a small woven platform where one tall, slender moth-winged being stands at the eyepiece, tiny in frame.
3) THEIR CITY AT NIGHT: clusters of soft gold and pale green lights on floating reef-islands across the dark sea, linked to each other by fine luminous threads, mist between them.
4) THE NURSERY: inside a woven, nest-like chamber of pale fibres, three small fuzzy moth-like young asleep together, lit only by the soft golden glow of an eye-spot pattern on a wing that curls protectively over them.
